# Make it usable

**Claude Community Berlin · Claude Workshop x The Delta · 4 December 2026 · session led by Yasaman Zahiri**

Three apps, each built in a weekend with AI. They look great. Nobody uses them.

Pick one with your team and turn it into something people would actually use. Think like a product manager: who is it for, what problem does it solve, what is essential, and what should go?

Each app is a single `index.html` file. To work on one with Claude Code, download it (or clone this repo), open the folder, and start `claude`.

## Five questions before you build

1. Who is it for? One specific person, not "everyone".
2. What problem do they have today, and how do they solve it now?
3. What is the one thing the product must do well?
4. What can we cut, so that one thing shines?
5. How will we know it works?

At the end, every team demos what they made: a paper sketch, a prototype or a rebuilt app all count.

All three apps are fictional and made on purpose for this workshop. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
