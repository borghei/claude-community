Five questions before you build

  1. Who is it for? One specific person, not "everyone".
  2. What problem do they have today, and how do they solve it now?
  3. What is the one thing the product must do well?
  4. What can we cut, so that one thing shines?
  5. How will we know it works?

Prompts to steal

Find the user

Act as an experienced product manager. Here is an app: [paste screenshots or describe it]. Ask me 5 questions, one at a time, to find out who this app is really for and what problem it solves for them.

Be the user

Play [the person we picked]. I will interview you about how you handle this problem today. Answer honestly, including what annoys you.

Keep, cut, change

Here are the app's features: [list]. Our user is [one sentence]. Sort every feature into Must have, Nice to have and Won't have, with one reason each. Be ruthless: at most 2 must-haves.

First 10 seconds

A friend just sent you this app. Here is a screenshot. Think out loud: what do you understand in the first 10 seconds, what would you tap, and what would make you close it?

Prototype it

Build a simple clickable prototype of only this flow: [your flow]. One main action per screen, plain language, and a 2-step onboarding with a skip button.

Rebuild it (Claude Code)

This folder has the app's index.html. Turn it into a focused app for [user] doing [job]. Remove everything else, make that job the home screen, and add a skippable 2-step onboarding.
